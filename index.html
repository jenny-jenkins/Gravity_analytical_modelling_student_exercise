Peak anomaly of your model, step by step

The peak is directly above the body, at x = 0. Write the units at every step. 1 mGal = 10⁻⁵ m/s².

    The equations and the model

    Buried sphere

    Δg = 4πG Δρ R³ z3 (x² + z²)3/2

    Radius R, depth to centre z. Half-width x½ = 0.766 z, so z ≈ 1.3 x½.

    Buried horizontal cylinder

    Δg = 2πG Δρ R² zx² + z²

    Infinitely long, at right angles to the profile. Half-width x½ = z.

    Buried vertical cylinder (on its axis)

    Δg = 2πG Δρ (L + S1 − S2)

    S1 = √(R² + zt²) and S2 = √(R² + (zt + L)²) are the slant distances from the point above the centre to the top and bottom edges. zt is the depth to the top, L the length.

    G = 6.67 × 10⁻¹¹ m³ kg⁻¹ s⁻²; Δρ is the density contrast with the host rock. Off its axis, the vertical cylinder is computed by summing thin vertical columns (matches the on-axis equation to 0.3 %). For scale: an infinite slab 100 m thick with Δρ = 300 kg/m³ gives 2πGΔρt = 1.26 mGal, so bodies a few tens of metres across at 100–200 m depth give tenths of a mGal.
